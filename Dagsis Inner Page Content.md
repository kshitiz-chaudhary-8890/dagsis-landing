## **INDUSTRY DETAIL PAGE: RETAIL** 

**Industry name:** Retail

**Sub-heading:** Turn every product question into a sale, day or night.

**Introduction**  
Shoppers ask the same things again and again: Is it in stock? How much is delivery? Can I return it? When customers don't get an answer quickly, they leave for another store. Dagsis gives your retail business an AI agent trained on your catalogue, policies and promotions, so every shopper gets an instant, accurate reply on WhatsApp, your website or social media.

**Use cases**

* Product enquiries: The agent answers questions about features, sizes, colours, pricing and availability using your product information.  
* Order and delivery questions: Customers get quick answers on shipping timelines, delivery fees, returns and exchanges.  
* Promotions and recommendations: The agent shares current offers and helps shoppers find what suits them.  
* Lead capture: When a shopper shows buying interest, the agent collects their contact details and saves them as a lead for your sales team.

**FAQs**  
 Q1. Can the agent answer questions about my specific products?  
 Yes. You upload your product details, price lists or website, and the agent answers from that information.

Q2. What if a customer asks something the agent doesn't know?  
 It won't guess. You can review the conversation in your chat history and add the missing information to your knowledge base.

Q3. Can it work on WhatsApp?  
 Yes. You can deploy the same agent to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and your website.

Q4. Will it help me collect customer details?  
 Yes. When it detects a potential buyer, it asks for their details and stores them in your Leads section.

**Banner heading:** Everything You Need to Run AI Customer Conversations.

**Banner sub-heading:** Build, deploy, manage and measure your AI agents from one simple dashboard.

**Feature 1: Workspace**  
 Your workspace is the home for everything your business does on Dagsis. Your agents, knowledge bases, conversations, team members and settings all sit together in one organised, secure place. 

**Feature 2: Team Members**  
 Invite colleagues into your workspace by email and give them edit or write permissions so they can help manage the platform, such as updating knowledge bases and agents. Your business admin keeps overall control.

**Feature 3: Role-Based Access**  
 You stay in control. Business admins manage members, update login details, assign roles and set permissions, while team members work with the edit or write access they have been given. Security controls help keep unauthorised people out of your workspace.

**Feature 4: Knowledge Base**  
 Teach your AI using your own content. Upload files, add URLs or connect your website, and Dagsis organises it into a knowledge base your agents learn from. Because agents answer from your knowledge, replies stay accurate and on-brand.

**Feature 5: Agents**  
 Create AI agents from your knowledge bases and shape how each one behaves. Set its role, tone of voice and behaviour through simple prompts, whether you want a friendly sales assistant or a formal support agent. You can create different agents for different purposes.

**Feature 6: Leads**  
 When the AI detects a potential buyer on any channel, it politely asks for their name, email, phone number and other details and saves them as a lead in your dashboard. Your sales team can see who is interested and follow up promptly.

**Feature 7: Analytics**  
 See how well your AI is performing. Track conversation volumes and how enquiries are being handled, and spot where your agent needs more knowledge. Use these insights to keep improving.

**Feature 8: Session and Chat History**  
 Every conversation is recorded and organised into sessions. Review full chat histories to understand what customers ask and check the quality of the AI's replies.

**Feature 9: Multiple Channels**  
 Deploy the same agent to your website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Discord and Telegram, and manage every conversation from a single dashboard.
